### Research Focus

My research centers on **Adversarial Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)**:

* **Adversarial Attacks & Threat Models:** Investigating vulnerabilities in cooperative and competitive MARL systems under state, action, and inter-agent communication perturbations.
* **Robustness & Defense Guarantees:** Designing provable and empirical defense mechanisms against Byzantine and adversarial agents in Decentralized Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (Dec-POMDPs).
* **Applied Testbeds:** Exploring robust multi-agent coordination in critical distributed systems, including autonomous satellite constellations and next-generation networks.

  <div class="authors"><strong>Hamza Ba-mohammed</strong>, Latifa El Bouga, Amine Andam, Mostapha Essoulami, Douae Benhlima, Jamal Bentahar, Mustapha Hedabou</div>

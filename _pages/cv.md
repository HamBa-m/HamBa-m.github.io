## Research & Professional Experience

### **Doctoral Researcher**
* **Institution:** College of Computing, UM6P, Morocco
* **Period:** 2024 – Present
* Theoretical and empirical investigation of adversarial attacks, Byzantine robustness, and defense algorithms in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL).
* First author of the comprehensive survey *“A Survey on Adversarial Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning”* (*under major revision at ACM Computing Surveys*).

### **AI Research Intern (Engineering Capstone)**
* **Lab:** [AI Movement — International AI Center of Morocco](https://aim.um6p.ma/en/home/), UM6P, Rabat
* **Period:** Feb 2024 – Aug 2024
* Modeled collaborative target acquisition and coverage optimization in Agile Earth Observation Satellite (AEOS) constellations as a Decentralized Markov Decision Process (Dec-POMDP).
* Extended and maintained the PyLEO simulation environment; evaluated DQN, DDPG, and MADDPG multi-agent algorithms under observation and communication constraints.

### **Research Assistant Intern**
* **Lab:** [AI Movement](https://aim.um6p.ma/en/home/), UM6P, Rabat
* **Period:** Jun 2023 – Oct 2023
* Investigated decentralized reinforcement learning models for distributed nano-satellite networks in low Earth orbit.

### **Data Scientist Intern**
* **Company:** Architeo (Remote)
* **Period:** Sep 2022 – Oct 2022
* Built conversational NLP models using the RASA framework with automated MLOps pipelines.

### **Software Engineer Intern**
* **Institution:** [National Center of Energy, Nuclear Sciences and Nuclear Technologies (CNESTEN)](https://www.cnesten.org.ma), Kenitra, Morocco
* **Period:** Jun 2022 – Aug 2022
* Engineered a supervisory real-time telemetry system for Radiation Portal Monitors (RPM) at nuclear facility checkpoints using TCP/IP networking, IoT microcontrollers, and full-stack interfaces.
